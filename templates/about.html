Across all {{ benchmark.sample_size }} Mark verses ({{ benchmark.total_tokens }} scoring tokens), Claude agreed with Berean's scholarly glosses on {{ "%.1f" | format(benchmark.agreement_rate * 100) }}% of tokens ({{ benchmark.matched_tokens }} / {{ benchmark.total_tokens }}). Recorded {{ benchmark.run_at }} using {{ benchmark.model }}.

The remaining ~{{ ((1 - benchmark.agreement_rate) * 100) | round(0, 'floor') | int }}% of non-matches are mostly translation-word-choice differences between Berean and WEB (the two English translations involved) — e.g. Berean “crowd” vs. WEB “people” for the same Greek ὄχλος — rather than alignment errors.

{{ t('about.h2_inspiration') }}

The parallel-columns concept and the green / red variant underlining (minor vs. major) are inspired by the bible-mt5 parallel viewer built by Dr. Zhan Chen (Associate Professor, Digital Social Science & Associate Distinguished Research Fellow at the Research Centre for History and Culture, United International College — BNU-HKBU UIC, Zhuhai). Dr. Chen's own scholarship focuses on Syriac biblical texts (dissertation: An Investigation into the Peshitta of Isaiah, Harvard NELC, 2020) and Chinese Bible translations.
