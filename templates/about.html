About the Translation Aligner

A focus-verse viewer for the Gospel of Mark across three textual witnesses — Greek NT, Syriac Peshitta, and Latin Clementine Vulgate — with word-level alignment and a scholarly critical apparatus.

Methodology

Every verse in Mark has been aligned word-by-word across the three witnesses by Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4.5 via the Batch API. Each alignment group records a variant verdict — aligned, minor, major, omitted, or added — plus a semantic type (agreement, construction, harmonisation, substitution, idiom, etc.) and, for every non-aligned group, one to two sentences of scholarly prose suitable for a critical apparatus.

Results are pre-computed and stored as per-verse JSON files in the repository — the running viewer has no runtime LLM dependency.

Viewer inspiration

The three-view layout (interlinear, alignment grid, critical apparatus) and the token-level variant highlighting are inspired by Professor Zhang Chen's bible-mt5 parallel viewer at University of International Business and Economics, Beijing.

Versification

Verse labels follow NA28 numbering. The Clementine Vulgate uses a different verse split in some chapters; where necessary (notably Mark 9 and Mark 4:40–41) we've remapped the Vulgate text to the NA28 boundaries. See the repository's known-issues.md for the complete list of edits.
